Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

Yes! We offer free shipping on all orders above ₹15,000 across India. For orders below that amount, a nominal shipping charge of ₹499 applies. Delivery usually takes 7–12 business days depending on your location.
We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you can return it in its original condition. Custom-made or made-to-order items are non-returnable. Please contact our support team to initiate a return.
Yes, free professional installation is included with most of our furniture. Our trained technicians will assemble and place the furniture at your preferred location. For some smaller items, simple self-assembly tools and instructions are provided.
All FurniHaven products come with a 5-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Upholstery has a 2-year warranty. The warranty covers structural issues but does not cover normal wear and tear, improper use, or damage caused by accidents.
Absolutely! We offer customization options for many products including fabric choice, wood finish, size adjustments, and more. Contact our design team via the Contact page or call us to discuss your requirements and get a custom quote.
